# Free AI E-Books

A collection of freely available AI and machine learning e-books.

## Deep Learning

- **Dive into Deep Learning (2nd ed.)** — https://d2l.ai/d2l-en.pdf
  Over 3,000 pages, runnable notebooks in PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow. CC BY-SA licensed.
- **Deep Learning** (Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville) — https://www.deeplearningbook.org/
  The classic theory reference, free in HTML. Predates transformers.
- **Neural Networks and Deep Learning** (Michael Nielsen) — https://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/
  Gentle first-principles introduction with visual backpropagation walkthroughs.

## Curated Lists

- **Awesome Free AI Books** (GitHub) — https://github.com/open-machine-learning-course/open-machine-learning-course
  Only links to official author or publisher sources. Covers deep learning, reinforcement learning, probabilistic ML, NLP, and LLMs.

## Reinforcement Learning & Probabilistic ML

- **Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction** (Sutton & Barto) — http://incompleteideas.net/book/bookdraft2018jan1.pdf
- **Probabilistic Machine Learning** (Kevin Murphy) — https://probml.github.io/pml-book/
  Drafts available free.

## LLM Foundations

- **Northeastern & NiuTrans — LLM Foundations** (Nov 2026) — highlighted by MIT CSAIL. Search "NiuTrans LLM foundations book" for the current link.

## Local AI

- **Local AI and Small Language Models** (Manning, free with subscription) — https://www.manning.com/books/local-ai-and-small-language-models
  Four chapters: small LMs, LoRA/DoRA specialization, GGUF + llama.cpp CPU inference, Ollama Python. 97 pages.
